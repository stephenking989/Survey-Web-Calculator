Blue border = you entered it. Green = calculated. Type over a green value to make it a known.
Working
S² = R² + V² (right triangle: radius across, vertical up, slant along the tape).  H = V − d.  Tilt of the tape from vertical: θ = atan(R / V).
Use the R and d values from your antenna's spec sheet (NGS antenna calibrations or the manufacturer). H is to the ARP; the phase-centre offset is applied by the receiver or office software.
